A note on flashing

SW Carousel does not flash on its own: a slide change on the lit projector is one dark gap, and a projector changes at most once a second. But at a short Time (under about 0.25 s), pressing Take or moving the Opacity fader quickly cuts back and forth, and rapid cuts can flash more than three times a second, which can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Nothing on this page presses anything for you; the line under the picture says when Time is that short.
